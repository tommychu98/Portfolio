TitleLine: optional block line wrapper for normal or highlighted lines.
TitleHighlight: inline word/phrase or full-line highlight; as defaults to span and supports em.

  Two brands,<br/>
  <TitleHighlight as="em">two directions.</TitleHighlight>
</HighlightTitle>
